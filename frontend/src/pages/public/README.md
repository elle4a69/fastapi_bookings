# Public Surface: Single-Page Website, Marketplace & High-Converting Onboarding (`frontend/src/pages/public`)

The public pages module implements the customer-facing acquisition, branding, booking discovery, and public tenant self-service onboarding surfaces for **FastAPI Bookings**.

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

The public module owns:
1. **Public Single-Page Website** (`/site` or `/site/:subdomain`): A responsive, branded website generated by the business owner via the Website Builder.
2. **Embedded Booking Engine**: Directly embedded within `/site` or `/book`, allowing visitors to book appointments without navigating away.
3. **Umbrella Marketplace & Geo-Radius Directory** (`/directory`): Marketplace directory with map discovery, distance calculation from visitor postcode/suburb, and instant clinic booking links.
4. **Public Self-Service Sign-Up & Pricing** (`/join`, `/signup`, `/register`, `/pricing`): Ultra-modern, animated 3-step onboarding wizard for new practice owners with plan selection, live subdomain slug availability verification, and immediate 14-day trial provisioning.
5. **Cryptographic Invitation Redemption Flow** (`/invite`, `/invite/accept`, `/invitations/accept`): Single-use invitation acceptance page with personalized greeting, tenant attribution, and one-click ownership claim.
6. **Proactive Chat & Greeting Widgets**: Floating visitor greeting banners with quick intent chips.

This module deliberately avoids exposing backend administrative endpoints, internal calendar notes, or private customer records to unauthenticated visitors.

## 3. Setup, Configuration & Dependencies

### Runtime Dependencies
- **React & TypeScript**: Client-side rendering and responsive layout primitives.
- **Framer Motion**: Smooth enter animations, layout spring physics, and step wizard transitions.
- **Lucide Icons**: Visual icon accents for features, plans, verification badges, and form inputs.
- **Mapbox GL / OpenStreetMap**: Geospatial map rendering and pin clustering.
- **Backend Endpoints**:
  - `GET /api/public/check-subdomain`: Real-time debounced slug availability verification.
  - `POST /api/public/signup`: Real tenant creation, founding owner provisioning, trial projection setup, and JWT token issue.
  - `GET /api/auth/invitations/{token}`: Public metadata inspection for single-use tenant invitations.
  - `POST /api/auth/invitations/accept`: Cryptographic invitation redemption and account activation.

## 4. Core Workflows & Contracts

### 4.1 High-Converting 3-Step Sign-Up Wizard
1. **Step 1: Choose Your Plan**:
   - Monthly vs. Annual toggle with animated -20% annual discount badge.
   - 3 Interactive Tier Cards:
     - **Starter** ($29/mo or $23/mo): Solo practitioners, 1 provider, 1 location.
     - **Growth** ($79/mo or $63/mo): Multi-provider (up to 5), 3 locations, 2-way SMS assistant, 3 addon modules, recommended glow border.
     - **Scale / Unlimited** ($199/mo or $159/mo): Unlimited providers/locations, Autonomous Voice AI Assistant Studio, Chatwoot CRM, white-labeling, priority SLA.
2. **Step 2: Workspace & Business Details**:
   - Business name with instant slug derivation.
   - Live availability badge (`/api/public/check-subdomain`).
   - Subdomain preview pill (`https://[subdomain].bookme.app`).
   - Industry selector and optional SMS phone.
3. **Step 3: Founding Owner Account**:
   - Owner first/last name, work email, password with strength indicators.
   - Submits to `POST /api/public/signup`.
   - Multi-step loading indicator, sets access token, updates active tenant, and redirects to `/admin/dashboard`.

### 4.2 Cryptographic Invitation Redemption
1. Target accesses `/invite?token=[token]`, `/join?token=[token]`, or `/invitations/accept?token=[token]`.
2. Inspects token via `GET /api/auth/invitations/{token}`.
3. Displays personalized workspace greeting card, role badge (`OWNER`, `ADMIN`), and verified email.
4. Invitee enters name and password, then submits to `POST /api/auth/invitations/accept`.
5. Authenticates immediately and launches `/admin/dashboard`.

### 4.3 Website Builder & Live Rendering
- **Templates**: Modern Minimalist, Wellness & Sanctuary, Clinical & Medical, Luxury & Boutique.
- **Themes**: Ocean Slate, Emerald Oasis, Rose Gold, Royal Indigo, Monochrome.
- **Embedded Booking Modes**: Native FastAPI Booking Form or headless Cal.com embed.
- **Checkout Estimation**: Dynamically computes out-call travel requirements, rendering transit time and travel fee quotes inline.

## 5. Data Safety & Isolation

- **Unauthenticated Public Scoping**: Public endpoints strictly isolate data to public fields (`name`, `bio`, `public_services`, `location_city`); internal notes, financial records, and staff schedules are completely omitted from responses.
- **Cryptographic Token Verification**: Single-use invitation tokens expire after 7 days, are marked `accepted` upon redemption, and cannot be replayed.
- **Tenant Isolation**: Self-service signups allocate dedicated tenant IDs and restrict subdomain collision across all tenants.
